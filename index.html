FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the ₹499 check-up a complete examination?

Yes. It includes a comprehensive oral exam, digital X-ray (if required), cavity and gum screening, a treatment plan consultation, and oral hygiene guidance.

How do I book an appointment?

Fill out the appointment form above and our team will call you back to confirm. You can also call us directly at +91 98765 43210.

Is root canal treatment painful?

Not with modern techniques. We use advanced anaesthesia and gentle techniques so most patients feel little to no discomfort during the procedure.

How long does a dental implant take?

The implant placement itself is a single procedure. Full healing and the final crown typically take about 3 to 6 months, depending on your individual case.

Do you offer payment plans or EMI options?

Yes. We offer flexible payment plans and EMI options for major treatments like implants and orthodontics. Speak to our front desk for details.
